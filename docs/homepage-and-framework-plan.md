# Website presentation and subsequent execution phase

## Public destinations

- `index.html`: trial-first homepage, idea-to-business narrative, short office-route explanations and four illustrative workflows with static printable sample documents.
- `services.html`: existing detailed catalogue, prices, managed and owned builders, family sites and assessment. This is a migration of the working catalogue, ready for the next content/pricing pass.
- `workspace.html`: existing authenticated dashboard and explicit request submission.

The homepage uses the established 3D character artwork and user-triggered animated conversation examples, not newly produced 3D video. Playback can be paused; reduced-motion mode shows the complete example. All sample businesses, requests and outputs are fictional and labelled. Sample pages can be printed to PDF using the browser; they do not execute tasks.

## Trial

The seven-day offer remains manually accepted and capped at two small deliverables from distinct departments. Add the Research launch brief: at most two pages, one product/service, one market, supplied facts and explicit assumptions; up to three public sources where useful. No sales forecast, paid research or full feasibility study.

## Annual offer for the pricing pass

Owner-approved direction: 20% discount on an agreed annual managed-service price, paid in four equal installments at commencement and months 3, 6 and 9. It is not a discount on custom framework builds, advertising spend or third-party running costs. Before an annual quote or checkout is implemented, define calendar-year allowances and the comparison basis, together with cancellation and scope-change terms. Existing 28-day builder amounts must not be multiplied by twelve and labelled a calendar year. No annual entitlement or billing changes are part of this presentation update.

## Connected framework after website work

Telegram is intended for internal coordination and the operational control channel. WhatsApp is the planned client-facing channel for enquiries, requests, updates and delivery links. No contact URL is invented or published before the owner supplies it.

The VPS can host long-running workflow workers and orchestration. MCP is an interface for authorized tools, not the scheduler or execution framework itself; the framework may use MCP tools. API credits and a VPS alone do not establish live integrations.

Later acceptance tests should cover a real request from intake through scoped execution, review, client-owned file storage, recorded delivery and client notification. Website events and invoice/email workflows also need duplicate-event protection, approval rules and failure reporting. Client-owned Drive or other storage, provider authorizations, retention, isolation and access revocation must be implemented before being advertised as connected.

Local page/link and behavior checks are performed now; end-to-end delivery tests belong to the subsequent connected-framework phase. Browser/mobile visual QA remains outstanding in this environment.
