The managed builder keeps existing 28-day pricing and adds a proposed annual option: 365 days, 13 cycle allowances, comparison 13 × standard cycle price, 20% discount, four equal payments at the start and months 3/6/9. The extra day does not add another allowance. Allowance scheduling and cancellation/refund terms require a written quote. This is a planning estimate, not checkout or activation.

Managed annual/cycle choice, selected departments, workload and estimates remain in the generated brief. Company-owned builds retain their separate milestone structure. Trial requests retain validated service IDs. All three continue via the existing tab-scoped pending-brief mechanism; clients sign in and explicitly review/save a workspace request. No provider connections or paid entitlements are created here.
