## Clip 1 / Your managed digital office / about 25 seconds

0–5s: A person at home sketches a business idea. The page opens into the familiar 3D office, with Maya welcoming them. Caption: “Your idea. A team behind it.”
5–12s: Maya receives a short request. A document specialist organizes a presentation, a research specialist prepares a clearly labelled launch brief, and a creative specialist lays out a social caption draft. All output is sample content.
12–19s: Maya presents draft previews to the business owner. The owner reviews and approves; the workspace shows a review milestone rather than a fake completed live job.
19–25s: Return to Maya and the orange AC logo. Caption: “Try up to two small deliverables free.” End: “7-day trial · No card · Scope confirmed first.”

Voiceover: “You bring the idea. Meet the digital office that helps you move it forward. Maya keeps the brief connected, with specialist roles for documents, research and creative work. Review the work, choose your next step, and start with a seven-day trial of up to two small deliverables.”

## Clip 2 / An office built for your company / about 25 seconds

0–6s: A business owner has separate tools, files and requests. The view becomes an organized office plan with the owner’s company identity.
6–14s: Maya and the technology specialist map an agreed workflow. Show stylized file storage, website and sender-account symbols with the caption “Connections scoped and authorized first.” Do not use fake live provider confirmations.
14–20s: The owner receives a framework plan, account ownership checklist and handover document. Highlight “Your systems · Your approved workflows · Agreed handover.”
20–25s: Orange Group logo and “Explore your own office framework.” End caption: “Scoped build · Running costs separate.”

Voiceover: “Want a digital office built around your company? Start with the work you need, then agree the systems, permissions and workflow. We scope the framework, your character identity and the handover together. Your company owns its agreed accounts and infrastructure, with running costs explained before the build.”
